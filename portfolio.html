Faith-forged football training, built by Coach Savion Williams. Dan designed the full website and brand identity — positioning Kingdom Training as elite, credible, and unmistakably its own.

A bold, faith-centered headline paired with real game-day photography and a scripture anchor (Colossians 3:23) — setting the tone immediately: elite training with purpose behind it.

One Link Medical Logistics

A healthcare logistics company needing a site that reads as trustworthy, fast, and compliant — the three things their clients care about most.

The Positioning

"Connecting Care. Delivering Trust."

A dark, tech-forward design with a live route line and glowing map — visually reinforcing what One Link actually does: connect patients, providers, and critical supplies with speed and precision. Reliable, Fast, Compliant, and Connected are called out immediately below the fold.
